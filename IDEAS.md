# Scoped idea slate

Do not lock the choice until the opening brief and judging criteria are known.

## 1. Proofline — recommended default

**Promise:** Turn messy operational evidence into one reviewable action, with every claim linked to its source.

Demo flow:

1. Drop in a synthetic invoice, email, voice note, or screenshot.
2. The agent creates normalized entities and detects one meaningful issue.
3. It proposes an action, cites the evidence, and asks for approval.
4. Approval produces an audit trail and a client-ready summary.

Why it is strong: the before/after is obvious, multimodal AI is useful rather than decorative, Cala can be the entity layer, OpenAI can perform extraction/reasoning, and Entire can show how the agent-assisted build evolved.

Hard scope: one document type, one issue type, one action. Use synthetic records.

Pitch sentence: **“Most business AI stops at an answer. Proofline turns evidence into a safe action you can verify, approve, and replay.”**

## 2. SignalDesk — fastest path

**Promise:** Convert a messy customer conversation into a clean operational handoff in seconds.

Demo flow:

1. Paste a synthetic WhatsApp-style thread or short voice message.
2. Extract the customer, intent, urgency, commitments, and missing information.
3. Generate the next action and an approval-ready reply.
4. Show the structured record and evidence side by side.

Why it is strong: very fast to build, easy to understand, and resilient with local fixtures.

Hard scope: one conversation, one generated handoff, one approval action.

Pitch sentence: **“SignalDesk turns the conversation everyone forgets into the next action nobody misses.”**

## 3. SceneProof — visual wildcard

**Promise:** Turn photos of a real-world problem into a verified task plan and a visual preview of the fix.

Demo flow:

1. Upload two or three synthetic site/product photos.
2. Detect the issue and organize evidence by object/location.
3. Produce a prioritized plan.
4. Generate one clearly labeled visualization of the proposed result.

Why it is strong: highly visual and gives Fal a meaningful role, but it has more latency and reliability risk.

Hard scope: one scene class and one generated preview.

Pitch sentence: **“SceneProof turns a camera roll into a plan you can approve—and a result you can see before work starts.”**

## Choice rubric

Score each candidate from 1–5 after the opening:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Direct fit with announced challenge | 5 |
| Wow moment visible in under 30 seconds | 5 |
| Can be complete by lunch | 4 |
| Sponsor integration is real and necessary | 3 |
| Deterministic offline fallback is easy | 3 |
| Team has the skills/data needed | 2 |

Choose the highest weighted score. In a tie, choose the smaller build.
